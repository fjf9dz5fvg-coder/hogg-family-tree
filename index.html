How the lines connect

  • Hogg: George Hogg and Elizabeth Rutherford (m. Roxburghshire 1844) → George Hogg (b. Hawick, Scotland, c.1859, gardener, d. Bradford 1927), son of George Hogg, married Sarah Ann Fox (b. Aberford) at Bradford Parish Church in 1878 → Frank Hogg (c.1887–1916), mill overlooker, killed on the Somme → Francis (1912–1993) → Brian (1935–2001) → Julian Phillip → Dean.
  • Madden: Thomas Madden (bricklayer's labourer) and Ann, of 74 Pollard St, Bowling → Kathleen (b.1909, baptised Catholic) married Francis Hogg in 1934.
  • Myers (Mum's side): George Myers and Alice Stancliffe (m. North Bierley 1933) → Colin Myers (b. 1934; sister Vera, m. Peter Cooper) married Barbara Bentley (b. 1938, daughter of Harry Bentley and Louisa? MacIntosh) at Pudsey Parish Church in Wharfedale in 1959 → Carol E. (b. 1961), with brothers Nigel and Timothy → married Julian P. Hogg in Leeds in 1985.
  • Stafford: Robert (Walworth carrier) and Susannah → William Henry (b. Newington 1832, d.1916) → Benjamin Bates (1876–1953) → Alfred (1903–1981) → Sheila (b.1937).
  • Lodge: Isaac → Michael (c.1799–1868, m. Rachel Walker 1819) → Joseph (bapt. Flockton 1819, Huddersfield furniture maker) → Edward (c.1846–1910, Wakefield solicitor) → Joseph (c.1869–1901) → Catherine (1901–1980). Every link from Joseph to Catherine is now confirmed by a census or a marriage register naming the father.
  • Marsden: George → Thomas (bapt. Bradford 1809, m. Mary Parker 1829) → Samuel (1833–1905, Dudley Hill grocer) → Edna (c.1870–1950) → Catherine Lodge. Samuel's baptism and Edna's marriage both name the father.

To check next

  • George Hogg's roots in Hawick: probably George Rutherford Hogg, b. Hawick 19 April 1858, son of George Hogg and Elizabeth Rutherford. The 1861 and 1871 Hawick censuses on ScotlandsPeople would show whether that family moved to Bradford. Sarah Ann Fox's baptism in Aberford (about 1854) would name her parents.
  • Ann Madden's maiden name: on Kathleen's Catholic baptism record (a paid Findmypast record) or Thomas and Ann's marriage (around 1894).
  • Myers line: George Myers's and Alice Stancliffe's own births, to take the line back. Also find Harry Bentley's marriage to Miss MacIntosh (1930s, Bradford area).
  • George Myers and Alice Stancliffe: their 1933 wedding isn't in the Church of England registers, so it was probably a chapel or register-office wedding. The marriage certificate would give both fathers' names.
